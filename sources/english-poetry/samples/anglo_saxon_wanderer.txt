# Exeter Cathedral Library MS 3501: The Wanderer (Exeter Book, folios 76b–78a)
# Metrical Form: Anglo-Saxon Alliterative Half-Lines (Sievers Type A/B/C) with Medial Caesura

Oft him anhaga | are gebideð,
metudes miltse, | þeah þe he modcearig
geond lagulade | longe sceolde
hreran mid hondum | hrimcealde sæ,
wadan wræclastas. | Wyrd bið ful aræd!

Swa cwæð eardstapa, | earfeþa gemyndig,
wraþra wælsleahta, | winemæga hryre:
"Oft ic sceolde ana | uhtna gehwylce
mine ceare cwiþan. | Nis nu cwicra nan
þe ic him modsefan | minne durre
sweotule asecgan." |

[Glossary & Translation Notes]
anhaga (m): solitary one, recluse
wyrd (f): fate, destiny, providence
eardstapa (m): earth-stepper, wanderer
wælsleaht (m): slaughter, mortal stroke
sweotule (adv): openly, clearly, plainly
